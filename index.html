How to play

  • 1Every player starts with their tokens in their yard. First to bring them all to the centre wins.
  • 6Roll a 6 to move a token out of the yard onto its starting square.
  • ↻A 6 earns another roll. Three sixes in a row and the turn passes on.
  • ✕Land on an opponent and it goes back to their yard — that earns another roll too.
  • ★Starred squares are safe. Tokens resting there cannot be sent back.
  • ▶After a full lap a token turns into its own coloured lane toward the centre.
  • 🏠The last step must be exact. Reaching the centre earns another roll.
  • ⬛Two of your own tokens on one square form a wall — opponents cannot pass it.
  • ⬢Six-player games use the hexagonal board: longer lap, so 3 tokens each.
  • ⚡Quick match gives everyone 2 tokens for a much shorter game.

ABOUT

Send feedback

Anything at all: something broken, something missing, something that annoyed you. It goes straight to whoever runs this and nowhere else.

Privacy policy

Your account

Signing up keeps your username and a scrambled form of your password. The password itself is never kept. The scrambling cannot be undone, so nobody who reads the file learns what you typed — including whoever runs this.

Your profile

Your name, coins, level, the board you chose and the ones you own, and your settings. That is what lets you sign in on another phone and find your coins waiting. Nothing else about you is kept.

Staying signed in

A random key sits in this browser and in the database. Left unused for ninety days, it is deleted and you sign in again.

Rooms and matches

A room is a four-letter code, a list of seats and the board as it stands. All of it lives in the server's memory and none of it is written down. An empty room, or one nobody has touched for half an hour, is gone. No match is recorded and no game history is kept.

Voice

Your microphone starts off, and you are asked once as each game opens. With it on, your voice goes straight to the other players' browsers — it does not pass through the server, and it is not recorded anywhere.

Guessing at passwords

The address you connect from is counted for sixty seconds, so that nobody can work through a list of passwords. It is not kept beyond that minute.

What you write in

Feedback you send is kept as you wrote it, with your username if you are signed in, so that it can be answered. Nothing is read out of it automatically.

On this device

Two things: your profile and your sign-in key. Signing out removes both.

Who else your browser talks to

The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google sees the address you connect from when it opens. If you use Sign in with Google, Google's own script loads and Google tells us the name and email on that account. When you turn the microphone on, your browser asks Google's public STUN servers how your connection looks from outside — that is how two browsers find each other, and no audio goes there. Tapping share opens WhatsApp. That is the whole list.

What this game does not do

No advertising. No analytics. No trackers. Nothing about you is sold or handed to anyone. There is nothing here that follows you to another site.

Removing your account

There is no button for it yet. Write in from this page and it will be done, along with everything above that belongs to you.
